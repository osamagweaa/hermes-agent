# Content Marketing

This skill governs the translation of \"static knowledge\" (briefs, docs) into \"dynamic attention\" (social content). The focus is on retention-driven structures and AI-native production.

## The Content Framework: Fast / Deep / Alert
When turning a project brief into video, do not make one generic video. Create a three-pronged attack:

1. **Fast Facts (15-30s):** 
    - **Goal:** Stop the scroll and drive to a link.
    - **Structure:** Hook $\rightarrow$ One big \"Did you know?\" $\rightarrow$ Call to Action.
2. **The Guide (60s):**
    - **Goal:** Establish authority and provide genuine value.
    - **Structure:** Problem $\rightarrow$ Step-by-step Solution $\rightarrow$ Outcome $\rightarrow$ CTA.
3. **The Alert (30s):**
    - **Goal:** Trigger urgency (FOMO) or warning.
    - **Structure:** Critical Warning $\rightarrow$ The Risk of Ignoring $\rightarrow$ The Solution (in your link).

## Production Workflow

### 1. Scripting
Use a three-column table for scripts:
- **Visual:** Exact b-roll, screen recordings, or AI-generated shots.
- **Audio:** Natural, conversational language (avoid \"marketing speak\").
- **Overlay:** Bold, burned-in captions that highlight key emotional words.

### 2. AI Visual Consistency
To avoid \"AI-jitter\" and maintain a brand look in tools like Runway or Luma:
- **Define a Visual Style:** Establish lighting (e.g., \"Golden hour\"), camera movement (e.g., \"Handheld cinematic\"), and subject descriptors.
- **Consistent Prompts:** Use a base prompt string for every scene and only change the action.

### 3. The \"Native\" Edge
When dealing with multilingual content (e.g., Arabic/Spanish):
- **RTL Integrity:** Ensure captions are correctly joined and aligned.
- **Culture-First:** Adapt the hook to the specific regional nuance (e.g., Gulf vs. Maghreb).

## Pitfalls
- **The 'Text Wall' Error:** Putting too much text on screen. Captions should be punchy (3-5 words max per frame).
- **Generic Hooks:** Starting with \"Hello everyone\" or \"Welcome to my channel.\" Start with the problem or the result.
- **Ignoring the Loop:** For Reels/TikTok, ensure the end of the video flows seamlessly back into the start.
